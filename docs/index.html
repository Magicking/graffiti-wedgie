How it works

This page runs the same views and controls as the wedgie app, drawn from the same 216 KB snapshot the app carries. Every graffiti is 128 × 128 pixels in one color, read straight from its contract’s storage. The demo has no live chain mode: on a wedgie that goes through its wedgie.dev page.
